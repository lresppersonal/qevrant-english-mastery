@distinct historic vs historical :: *Historic* means important in history (*a historic victory*); *historical* means connected with the past (*historical documents*, *a historical novel*).
@distinct critic vs criticism vs review :: A *critic* is a person; *criticism* is negative comment (or the activity of analysing art); a *review* is an article judging a book, film etc.
