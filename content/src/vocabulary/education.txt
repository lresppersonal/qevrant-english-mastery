@distinct learn vs teach vs study :: You *learn* something (gain knowledge); someone *teaches* you; you *study* a subject (spend time learning it, often formally). ~~She learned me English~~ is wrong.
@distinct university vs college vs school :: In British English *school* is for children (to 18); *college* often means further education or a part of a university; *university* awards degrees. In American English *school* and *college* can mean university.
