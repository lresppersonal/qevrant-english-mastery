@distinct misinformation vs disinformation :: Both are false information. *Disinformation* is spread on purpose to deceive; *misinformation* may be spread by people who believe it.
@distinct say vs tell vs speak vs talk :: *Tell* needs a person object (*tell me*); *say* doesn't (*say something*). *Speak* is more formal or about languages; *talk* suggests conversation.
