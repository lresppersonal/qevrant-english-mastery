@distinct invention vs discovery :: You *discover* something that already existed (a planet, a law of nature); you *invent* something new that did not exist before (a machine, a process).
@distinct technique vs technology :: A *technique* is a way of doing something (a surgical technique); *technology* is the machines and scientific knowledge used to do it.
