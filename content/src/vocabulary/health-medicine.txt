@distinct disease vs illness vs injury :: A *disease* is a specific medical condition, often infectious (*heart disease*); *illness* is the state of being unwell; an *injury* is physical damage from an accident.
@distinct cure vs treat vs heal :: You *treat* a patient or illness (try to make it better); you *cure* an illness (make it go away completely); wounds *heal*.
