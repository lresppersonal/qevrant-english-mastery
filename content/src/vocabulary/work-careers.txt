@distinct job vs work vs career :: *Work* is uncountable and general (*I have a lot of work*). A *job* is countable: a particular paid position (*She got a new job*). A *career* is the series of jobs in one field across your working life.
@distinct sack vs lay off :: You are *sacked* (*fired*) because of something you did wrong; you are *laid off* (*made redundant*) because the employer no longer needs your role.
