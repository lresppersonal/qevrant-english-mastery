@distinct tasty vs tasteful :: *Tasty* means having a good flavour. *Tasteful* means attractive and showing good judgement in style (*a tasteful interior*), not food.
@distinct cook vs cooker vs chef :: A *cook* or *chef* is a person (*chef* = professional). A *cooker* is a machine for cooking. ~~My mother is a good cooker~~ is wrong.
