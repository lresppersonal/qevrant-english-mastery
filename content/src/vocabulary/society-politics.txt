@distinct politics vs policy vs politician :: *Politics* is the activity of government and power (*interested in politics*). A *policy* is a particular plan (*a new transport policy*). A *politician* is a person in politics.
@distinct affect vs effect :: *Affect* is usually a verb (*the cuts affect the poor*); *effect* is usually a noun (*the effect of the cuts*). *Effect* as a verb means *bring about* (*effect change*).
