@distinct environmental vs environmentalist :: *Environmental* describes things connected with the natural world (*environmental damage*). An *environmentalist* is a person who works to protect it. Don't say ~~an environmental person~~.
@distinct climate vs weather :: *Weather* is the conditions on a particular day; *climate* is the typical pattern over many years. *Climate change* is about long-term patterns, so ~~a hot climate today~~ is wrong.
