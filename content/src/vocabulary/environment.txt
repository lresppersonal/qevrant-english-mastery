@distinct environmental vs environmentalist :: *Environmental* describes things connected with the natural world (*environmental damage*). An *environmentalist* is a person who works to protect it. Don't say ~~an environmental person~~.
@distinct climate vs weather :: *Weather* is the conditions on a particular day; *climate* is the typical pattern over many years. *Climate change* is about long-term patterns, so ~~a hot climate today~~ is wrong.

@distinct renewable vs sustainable :: *Renewable* energy comes from sources that are naturally replaced (sun, wind). *Sustainable* describes any practice that can continue without damaging future resources; not all renewable practices are sustainable.
@distinct conserve vs preserve :: To *conserve* is to use carefully so that something lasts (*conserve water*). To *preserve* is to keep something as it is, protecting it from change or damage (*preserve a habitat*).
