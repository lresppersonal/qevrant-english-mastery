@distinct sensible vs sensitive :: *Sensible* means having good judgement (*a sensible decision*). *Sensitive* means easily affected or aware of others' feelings (*a sensitive child*). Russian *сенситивный/чувствительный* = *sensitive*.
@distinct nervous vs anxious vs apprehensive :: *Nervous* is everyday worry, often physical (*nervous before an exam*). *Anxious* is stronger or longer-lasting. *Apprehensive* is formal and about something in the future.

@distinct intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation :: *Intrinsic* motivation comes from the activity itself (curiosity, enjoyment). *Extrinsic* motivation comes from outside rewards or pressures (grades, money, praise). Both are academic terms.
@distinct compulsive vs impulsive :: *Impulsive* behaviour happens suddenly without thinking (*an impulsive purchase*). *Compulsive* behaviour is repeated because a person feels unable to stop (*compulsive checking*).
