@distinct sensible vs sensitive :: *Sensible* means having good judgement (*a sensible decision*). *Sensitive* means easily affected or aware of others' feelings (*a sensitive child*). Russian *сенситивный/чувствительный* = *sensitive*.
@distinct nervous vs anxious vs apprehensive :: *Nervous* is everyday worry, often physical (*nervous before an exam*). *Anxious* is stronger or longer-lasting. *Apprehensive* is formal and about something in the future.
