## Determiners and quantifiers
a | indefinite article | неопределённый артикль (не переводится); один, какой-то | used before a singular countable noun that is mentioned for the first time or is not specific | She works at a university in Kazan. | Use "an" before a vowel sound: an hour, an idea, but a university. | an
the | definite article | определённый артикль (не переводится); этот, тот самый | used before a noun when both speaker and listener know which one is meant | The report you sent was very useful. | No article with general plural or uncountable nouns: Education matters (not "The education matters"). |
this | determiner, pronoun | этот, эта, это | the person or thing near you, or the one just mentioned | This approach has two main advantages. | Plural: these. Often used to refer back to a whole idea in academic writing. | these
that | determiner, pronoun, conjunction | тот; что, который | the person or thing further away or already mentioned; also introduces a clause | Many people believe that cities are safer now. | "That" can often be omitted after verbs like say, think, believe. | those
these | determiner, pronoun | эти | plural of this | These findings suggest a clear link. | | this
those | determiner, pronoun | те | plural of that | Those who failed were offered a second attempt. | "those who" = the people who. | that
some | determiner, pronoun | некоторые, несколько, немного | an amount or number that is not stated exactly | Some students prefer to study at night. | Usually in positive sentences; in questions when offering: Would you like some tea? | any
any | determiner, pronoun | любой; какой-нибудь; нисколько | used in questions and negatives for an unspecified amount; also "it does not matter which" | Are there any questions? / Any student can apply. | | some; anybody; anything
no | determiner | никакой, ни одного | not any | There is no evidence for this claim. | Stronger than "not any". | none
none | pronoun | ни один, никто, ничего | not one or not any of a group | None of the candidates was suitable. | Can take singular or plural verb: None of them is/are ready. | no
every | determiner | каждый, всякий | all the members of a group, seen one by one | Every student receives feedback. | Followed by a singular noun and verb. | each; everyone; everything
each | determiner, pronoun | каждый (в отдельности) | every one of two or more things, considered separately | Each paragraph should have one main idea. | Each can refer to two items; every needs three or more. | every
all | determiner, pronoun | все, весь | the whole number or amount of something | All the data were collected in 2025. | All + plural noun (general) or all the + noun (specific). |
both | determiner, pronoun | оба, обе | the two people or things together | Both options have drawbacks. | both … and: Both teachers and students agreed. | either; neither
either | determiner, pronoun, adverb | любой из двух; тоже (в отрицании) | one or the other of two; also "also" in negative sentences | You can choose either topic. / I don't like it either. | either … or for two alternatives. | neither; both
neither | determiner, pronoun | ни один из двух | not one and not the other of two | Neither solution is perfect. | neither … nor: Neither the students nor the staff were informed. | either
many | determiner, pronoun | много, многие | a large number of (used with plural countable nouns) | Many people believe that technology makes life easier. | Use many with countable nouns (many books) and much with uncountable nouns (much time). In positive sentences "a lot of" is more informal. Comparative: more, superlative: most. | more; most; much; a lot of
much | determiner, pronoun, adverb | много; гораздо, намного | a large amount of (used with uncountable nouns); also "a lot" before comparatives | We don't have much time. / This is much better. | Mainly in questions and negatives; formal in positive sentences: Much research has been done. | more; most; many
more | determiner, adverb | больше, более | a larger number or amount; used to form comparatives | More research is needed. / more expensive | Comparative of many and much. | many; much; most
most | determiner, adverb | большинство; самый | the largest number or amount; used to form superlatives | Most experts agree. / the most important factor | "most people" (general) vs "most of the people" (specific group). | many; much; more
few | determiner, pronoun | мало, немногие | not many (negative meaning) | Few students finished on time. | "a few" = some (positive): A few students finished early. | a few; fewer; little
a few | determiner | несколько, немного | a small number of (positive meaning) | I have a few ideas. | Compare: few = hardly any. | few
little | determiner, adjective | мало (неисчисл.); маленький | not much (with uncountable nouns); also small in size | There is little evidence of progress. | "a little" = some: a little time. | a little; less; least
a little | determiner, adverb | немного | a small amount of; slightly | Add a little salt. / I'm a little tired. | | little
less | determiner, adverb | меньше | a smaller amount (with uncountable nouns) | We should use less plastic. | Traditionally "fewer" with countable nouns: fewer cars, less traffic. | little; least; fewer
fewer | determiner | меньше (с исчисляемыми) | a smaller number of | Fewer people smoke than before. | | few; less
least | determiner, adverb | наименьший; меньше всего | the smallest amount; used to form negative superlatives | the least expensive option | at least = not less than: at least 250 words. | less; little
several | determiner, pronoun | несколько | more than two but not many | Several studies have reached the same conclusion. | | a few
enough | determiner, adverb | достаточно | as much or as many as needed | There isn't enough time. / Is it big enough? | Before nouns (enough money) but after adjectives (old enough). |
another | determiner, pronoun | ещё один; другой | one more; a different one | Can I have another try? | another + singular noun; other + plural. | other
other | determiner, adjective, pronoun | другой | different; additional; the rest | Other countries have tried this. | the other(s) = the remaining ones. | another; others
such | determiner | такой | of this kind; to such a degree | Such measures are expensive. / It was such a good idea. | such + (a) + adjective + noun; so + adjective alone. | such as
own | determiner, adjective | собственный | belonging to a particular person | She runs her own business. | Always after a possessive: my own, their own. |
whole | adjective, noun | весь, целый | all of something | The whole class passed. | the whole + singular noun; all + plural. |
various | adjective | различный, разный | several different | for various reasons | | variety
certain | determiner, adjective | некоторый; определённый; уверенный | particular but not named; also sure | Certain groups are more at risk. / I'm certain. | | certainly
plenty of | quantifier | много, полно | a large amount, more than enough | We have plenty of time. | Neutral/informal; positive sentences. |
a lot of | quantifier | много | a large number or amount | A lot of people use public transport. | Informal; in essays prefer many, much, a large number of. | lots of

she | pronoun | она | a female person already mentioned | She runs a small company. | Object form: her; possessive: her, hers. | her; hers; herself
we | pronoun | мы | the speaker together with others | We need clearer rules. | Object form: us; possessive: our, ours. | us; our; ours; ourselves
myself | reflexive pronoun | себя, сам | used when the subject and object are the same person, or for emphasis | I taught myself to code. | Other forms: yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves. | yourself; himself; herself; itself; ourselves; themselves

quite | adverb | довольно; вполне | fairly; completely (with strong adjectives) | quite good / quite right | Meaning depends on the adjective. |

## Auxiliary and modal verbs
be | verb | быть, являться | exist; used to describe; auxiliary for continuous and passive | She is a teacher. / It was built in 1900. | am, is, are; was, were; been; being. | am; is; are; was; were; been; being
have | verb | иметь; (вспомогательный) | possess; auxiliary for perfect tenses | I have two sisters. / They have finished. | has, had, having. | has; had; having
do | verb | делать; (вспомогательный) | perform; auxiliary for questions and negatives | Do you agree? / do research | does, did, done, doing. | does; did; done; doing
can | modal verb | мочь, уметь | be able to; be allowed to | She can speak three languages. | Past/conditional: could. | could; be able to
could | modal verb | мог бы; мог | past of can; possibility; polite request | It could be true. / Could you help? | | can
may | modal verb | может, возможно; можно | possibility; permission (formal) | This may cause problems. | Common hedging verb in academic writing. | might
might | modal verb | мог бы, может быть | weaker possibility | It might rain later. | | may
must | modal verb | должен; наверняка | necessity; logical certainty | You must register. / She must be tired. | Negative mustn't = prohibition; don't have to = no necessity. | have to
shall | modal verb | (для будущего, предложения) | future (formal); offers and suggestions | Shall we start? | Mostly in questions with I/we. |
should | modal verb | следует, должен бы | advice; expectation | Governments should invest more. | |
will | modal verb | (будущее время); буду | future; willingness | Prices will rise. | | would
would | modal verb | бы; (будущее в прошедшем) | conditional; past of will; polite | I would choose the first option. | | will
ought to | modal verb | следует, должен | should (slightly formal) | You ought to apologise. | |
need | verb, modal verb | нуждаться; нужно | require | You need to rest. / Need I say more? | |
used to | verb | раньше (бывало) | past habit or state that is no longer true | I used to live in Moscow. | Different from "be used to + -ing" (= accustomed). |
had better | modal phrase | лучше бы (следует) | advice with a warning | You'd better leave now. | Followed by the base verb. |
## Common verbs

bring | verb | приносить | carry towards | bring change | brought. | brought
